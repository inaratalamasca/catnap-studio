AIU INBOX PREVIEW FEATURES

  • Pending inbox entries, oldest first, with source preferences and warning flags.
  • Managed Suno/YouTube running order, including YouTube-only entries without placeholder songs.
  • Manual LP Load and Play. Pause cancels a pending start. Optional AP autoplay.
  • Private uploader notes and source-correct now-playing text with uploader @credit to copy, review and send yourself.

IN THE 0.8.0 PREVIEW

  • Radio from Suno or the AIU party player. Only the source tab shows On Air; the Theatre AIU logo turns green with a small listener link while live.
  • One gear opens Presentation controls, with Radio and Inbox buttons in that separate window.
  • The top status names Inbox or Suno playlist mode. Green means the linked Theatre is ready without current playback or control errors.
  • YouTube fills the Theatre window on Play, with a red YouTube badge and a clear Loaded label before playback.
  • Move upcoming Inbox songs up or down. Hold or release songs without losing your choices when the inbox refreshes.
  • Keep held songs out of the playlist. When an uploader’s song finishes, only their next song joins the current tail; later songs keep waiting.
  • Keep the managed playlist in played and upcoming order, including host moves and manual releases. Held songs and repeat uploaders are clearly marked.
  • Manually Decline a submission with “Only one song at a time, please.” Nothing is auto-declined.
  • Larger fitted artwork and a small playback-status line above the progress bar leave more room for the presentation.
  • Compact current-song cards show artwork, credits and warnings. Click the thumbnail to copy the public artwork link.
  • A clean Theatre + Inbox install offers stream-name setup. Upgrades and existing name preferences are not prompted.
  • Keep a token for the same relay across normal updates, or choose session-only or Forget.

Radio needs no desktop helper. Managed playlist reordering needs the companion bundled with0.8.0; existing Suno permissions stay the same. Radio uses individual host keys: ask an AIU Agent/Admin. Radio key manager ↗. Use one Radio publisher for a show.

The 0.8.0 packages are desktop Chrome/Brave development previews awaiting a real host rehearsal. The host confirmed0.6.10 statuses, managed lyrics and publishing with an approved individual key. New fair rotation, playlist order, Decline and advanced hosting still need a live rehearsal. The Chrome Web Store beta was submitted separately from these downloads.

The full hosting package adds show artwork and reviewed dark cue sheets; those are separate from the simple managed queue. First-party tools use noncommercial terms; commercial use outside those terms needs Ina’s written permission. Branding rights stay separate.
