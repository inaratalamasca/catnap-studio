Install Community

  1. Download the ZIP above and extract it to a folder you’ll keep.
  2. Open Extensions. Type chrome://extensions in Chrome or brave://extensions in Brave.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, then select the extracted folder that contains manifest.json.

Put on your first show

  1. Open a Suno playlist in the same browser profile and press Suno’s Play.
  2. Click Theatre on the page or the pinned Catnap icon. Use the gear to open Presentation controls.
  3. Pick a layout. Go big on artwork, read along with lyrics, or give the playlist and visuals their own spaces.
  4. Open the Media Board to save pictures, GIFs and muted videos as pads. Tap a pad to put it on the artwork or a side stage.

Make the background move

Under Background visualizer, choose Spectrum bars, Wave, Pulse rings, or Stars. Adjust the opacity, tint and reaction strength. The visuals follow playback and use audio levels when available.

Play a song’s linked video

If the current song’s public Suno description includes a YouTube video, choose Play linked YouTube video below the cover. Theatre pauses Suno and opens the embedded player. Choose Back to artwork to return.

What do I need?

Chrome or Brave on a computer, and a Suno playlist you can play. Community uses your existing Suno player and saves your settings and visuals in this browser.
