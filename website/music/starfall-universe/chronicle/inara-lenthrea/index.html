Inara and Lenthrea

Lavender snow. Emerald fire.

Meet the two Huntresses at the center of Starfall.

Inara Tal’amasca, Starfall

White fur, lavender rosettes, a long plush tail and a gaze that seems to finish the calculation before anyone else notices the danger.

Inara is a Prefect First Class with the elite Huntress designation. She tracks, investigates, rescues and fights. She reads the terrain, finds the angle and wastes no movement.

She is guarded, fiercely loyal and protective. The woman at the microphone and the woman enjoying an unhurried evening are Starfall, too.

Lenthrea, War-Tigress

Emerald and teal fur, dark stripes, cyan starlight in her markings, and a presence as capable of mischief as it is of shelter.

Len is also a S.T.A.R. Prefect Huntress, a singer and harpist, and Inara’s partner in love and in the field. In their shared cases, Inara often secures the pattern and the evidence while Len attends to the person inside the emergency. Both can strike. Both can listen.
