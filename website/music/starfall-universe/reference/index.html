Timeline & glossary

Trace the events described in the Chronicle and look up the terms used in the songs.

Timeline

Dates and the sequence of individual writs can be open in the stories.

  1. The old developed core

    Crowded, highly mediated core societies motivate intentional frontier settlement.

  2. Slowboat settlement

    Tal’Shyra founders accept remoteness to build people-first ecology and civic life. Kepler’s early working settlement develops through long freight routes. Their relative founding order is not pinned.

  3. The First Hoppers

    Accessible Kepler ore, pressure-hab camps, scarce personal mass and portable worker music. Big Bill’s barrel enters remembered space-grass origins.

  4. Kepler’s resource and travel revolution

    Mining and growing infrastructure enable faster travel, mature yards, freight, migration and tourism. Deep Road work belongs later than the First Hoppers.

  5. Tal’Shyra becomes reachable

    Welcoming worlds face growing development pressure; Kepler/Pilgrim effects are economic and historical here, not local gravitational damage.

  6. The Three Years

    Deniable mercenary warfare; guardian militias; Inara fights beside Zyara; Kael’Rath gravely wounded; elevators destroyed.

  7. The Three Days

    Lazaan’s intervention and coordinated squadron action break the mercenary campaign.

  8. Three days to leave

    Inara accepts academy offer and prepares for departure. Minimum service: eight local / ten galactic-standard years. Visits allowed after first academic year.

  9. Academy and early postings

    Inara becomes a Prefect, later Prefect First Class / Huntress. Early Kepler postings bring Junie, Rakkit and Drifter adjunct work during SnowClaw maintenance.

  10. The Varrik operation chain

    Snowblind Siege → neural severance and 97% reconstruction → Varrik.EXE → Shadow Vector containment strike.

  11. Ichigo joins the circle

    Frozen Orbit rescue precedes Dead Beacon Choir, in which Ichigo serves the team as marine combat communications specialist.

  12. Wide patrols and life between missions

    Solo deployments, maintenance, performance, hearings and meetings recur. Many songs predate Lenthrea; others occur during separate assignments.

  13. The two claws

    Inara and Lenthrea’s partnership; Tal’Shyra marriage referenced before The Honey-Moons of Kepler; honeymoon precedes return-to-duty Ride the Bent Sky.

  14. Rights and accountability cases

    Living Ledger, Shelterstone, Open Spectrum, Open Laurels and related missions develop mature S.T.A.R. law. Their exact inter-case order remains open.

  15. Fallow Light and the window

    One Chair for the Missing has no confirmed Tavi resolution; The Seat Beside the Window follows its aftermath in the dossier.

  16. Jenkins’s present introduction

    Ancient founding Prefect enters current Starfall action and fleet-command circle; The Walls Answer demonstrates coordinated hostage rescue.

Glossary

Accord Writ
Specific judicial authority for an operation; includes limits and survives later review.
Blackwater
The Pilgrim-wake field and operating culture of dangerous mineral/debris routes; navigation differs sharply from ordinary settled-space flying.
Counterbalance
Inara’s assignment to monitor Varrik’s non-zero residual risk after Snowblind.
Drifter
Specialty mining command, escort, tow and rescue vessel assigned to Inara’s Kepler adjunct work; distinct from SnowClaw.
First Hoppers
Early Kepler miners and working settlers associated with long routes, accessible ore, pressure habitats and communal space-grass.
Forest law
Inherited duty to protect life, preserve balance, exercise restraint and answer unjust violence; subject to the facts of a defense, not immunity by ancestry.
GhostTide
Lenthrea’s stealth-oriented Huntress cruiser, with sensor masking, gravity control, boarding and rescue functions.
Huntress
Rare elite Prefect designation; a specialization, not a species or license to act outside review.
Kepler Haven
A recurring cultural/hospitality name. Existing venue and local namesakes can appear in both Kepler and Shae’lo’ra settings; coordinates depend on the song.
Kepler-62
Frontier system of belts, industry, docks, settlements and leisure worlds; distinct from Shae’lo’ra.
Lumenweave
Pelagari integrated photophoric language of light, motion, posture and environmental context.
Pilgrim
Wandering black hole whose wake supplies the Kepler region’s mineral wealth and strange dangerous routes.
Prefect
S.T.A.R. Marshalate judicial field officer: investigator, responder, protector, pursuer and enforcer under writ.
Saelun
Chosen name of Cyravel’s emergent environmental intelligence, protected claimant in Shelterstone.
Shae’lo’ra
Mature cultural, trade, repair and nightlife system; also spelled Shaelo’ra or Shay’lo’ra in earlier material.
Skyroot
Tal’Shyran space elevator; a grounded connection to orbit and an image for the civic life around it.
Snjókló / SnowClaw
Recorded alternate vessel name and Common rendering for Inara’s extreme-operations battle cruiser. No unsupported phonetic standard is imposed.
Space-grass
Kepler’s portable worker/migrant instrument ecology, capable of bluegrass, progressive, Celtic, Nordic, industrial and local forms.
S.T.A.R.
Systems Treaty & Accord Republic: interstellar civic, legal and defensive framework.
Starfall
Inara Tal’amasca’s codename and genuine stage expression; also the universe’s organizing name.
Tal’amasca
Inara’s northern snow-leopard clan and family lineage on Tal’Shyra.
Tal’rashaan
Family/lineage associated with Lazaan and Bassan; not an invented species or automatic ship’s crew.
Tal’Shyra
Ecology-first blue/green/white homeworld of Inara, with plural biomes and multispecies civic life.
The Threes
Origin motif: three local years of war, three days of intervention, three days to prepare for academy departure.
The three percent
Symbol of unfinished risk from Varrik’s interrupted 97% transmission, not a settled probability or known portable fragment.
